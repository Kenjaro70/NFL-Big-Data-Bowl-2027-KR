## Which combine slots are route breaks

A slot counts if, in reps with ball tracking (≥ 10), its median apex comes no more than 0.5 s after the throw: the game rule. The throw is the first frame the ball moves faster than 8 yd/s. Slots without enough ball reps are dropped unless they are the same break logged under a second drill name. Then each cut also needs its apex ≥ 1.5 s into the rep (the game's entry-window rule), which removes most slant breaks, as it does in games.

## Positive control: does the pipeline find transfer where it should?

Combine: the highest smoothed speed in any tracked 40 rep (same Savitzky–Golay smoothing as the cut detector). Games: the mean, over a WR's regular-season go routes (≥ 10), of the route's peak smoothed speed from the snap to 1 s after the throw. Combine reliability: first vs second 40 rep (375 WRs with two reps), Spearman–Brown corrected; game reliability: split-half across games. Beyond the baseline (which already holds the 40 time), tracked top speed adds β = +0.33 SD per SD [-0.18, +0.77], p = 0.212.
